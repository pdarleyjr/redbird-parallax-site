One app for every Red Bird neighborhood event. Install it once and you’ll have the Halloween trail now, the holiday trail when it opens, and future events, even with weak signal on the night.

  • Find the nearest house

You’re using the Red Bird app ✓

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Keep Open as Web App turned on.
  4. Tap Add. Open Red Bird from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Install app above when it appears.
  2. Or open the Chrome menu (three dots) and tap Install app.
  3. Open Red Bird from your Home screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Look for the install icon (a screen with a down arrow) at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click it, then click Install.

Works offline once installed. Your location never leaves your phone.
